## What this repository is for

- **Indata:** third-party aerials and lab stills (not redistributed as a product)
- **Produkt:** analysis code (`tools/`), derived metrics (JSON/CSV), masks, plots,
  and documented negative/inconclusive results under `outputs/`

No commercial use of third-party images is intended or permitted.
Do **not** publish photographer originals in blogs, books, apps, merch, or
public forks. If results are published publicly, prefer **links to sources**
plus **our generated visualizations / tables**, not the raw aerials.
