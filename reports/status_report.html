A Friday-night forensic dive into crop formations: after the ~90% that are plank-and-rope hoaxes, is there anything measurable left? We answer with numbers and known-answer tests — and we debunk our own best cases when the data says so.

The stance

Measure first. No claims without validation.

Every estimator is validated against a known answer before it is trusted on real data. A finding only counts as "interesting" if it clears one of four bars:

bar 1 · bits

Independent bit recovery

Recovered message matches published plaintext at BER well below 0.25.

bar 2 · geometry

Ratios that replicate

Diameter/area ratios pass known-answer tests and hold up on perspective-corrected imagery.

bar 3 · lab

Cited lab metrics

Plant/soil numbers transcribed with citations that survive CICAP-style methodology critique.

bar 4 · control

A control separates

A known hoax (Chualar/NVIDIA) cleanly separates on features while candidates don't.

What we built

A reproducible, local toolkit

Pure-Python math cores (numpy/Pillow only) plus an OpenCV computer-vision layer. Everything runs offline; every module ships with tests.

forensics · 23/23

Hawkins ratios

Diatonic-scale + the five Euclidean crop-circle theorems (4:3, 4:1, 2:1). Validated on synthetic exact ratios.

forensics · 4/4

Fractal dimension

Box-counting + lacunarity, auto linear-region fit. Validated: Koch 1.26, Sierpinski carpet 1.89, square 2.0, line 1.0.

forensics · 11/11

Encoding verifiers

π spiral, Crabwood ASCII, Arecibo/Chilbolton diff, and the Julia-set classifier.

ccat · 3/3

Mask-first circle extraction

Contour+circularity, not raw Hough. Recovers a synthetic 150-circle log-spiral 150/150 (0% error).

ccat · 6/6

grid_analyze (GLYPH-style)

Entropy, 2D-FFT peakiness, periodicity, symmetry, and an "absence signal" z-score vs shuffled grids.

ccat · 3/3

Preprocess + spatial

Perspective rectify, excess-green stubble mask; haversine monument distances + pure-Python lunar phase.

Findings

What the numbers actually say

Two columns, because honesty cuts both ways. On the left, what held up. On the right, what we ruled out — including cases we'd hoped were anomalous.

✅ Held up / decoded

  • VERIFIEDBarbury Castle π (2008) — the ratcheted spiral decodes to 3.141592654, tenth digit correctly rounded.
  • DECODEDCrabwood (2002) — 8-bit ASCII: "Beware the bearers of FALSE gifts & their BROKEN PROMISES…" (ambiguous word + terminal flagged, not hidden).
  • STRUCTUREDArecibo / Chilbolton — 1679 = 23×73 semiprime; reply adds silicon (Z=14), triple helix, figure ≈101 cm, planets 3-4-5.
  • MATHWilton Windmill (2010) — Euler's identity e^(iπ)+1=0 in ASCII (a debated "h" glyph to re-check).

🗡️ Ruled out / downgraded

  • SUSPICIOUSCherhill "meteoritic iron" (1993) — reportedly iron filings from a hoax/art project ("Fe3"). Source is a blog, not peer-review → unverified, not debunked, but off the priority list.
  • HUMANStonehenge "Julia Set" (1996) — not a true z²+c fractal; a ~149–151 circle log-spiral. Rod Dickinson confessed to a ~2h45m overnight build.
  • NULLLogan / Eltopia (C3) — no usable aerial imagery exists publicly; the burned-crop question lives in lab data, not computer vision.
  • CATEGORY ERRORDNA / ancient-script "decoders" — crop circles have no real DNA or symbol corpus; the "DNA helix" is a shape. Skipped as pseudo-science.
