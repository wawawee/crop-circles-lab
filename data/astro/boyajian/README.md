# Boyajian's Star (TIC 272172248 / KIC 8462852) — Data Sources

## Background

Boyajian's Star (aka Tabby's Star, KIC 8462852, TIC 272172248) is an F-type
main-sequence star in Cygnus (~1470 ly, V≈11.7). It was observed by **Kepler**
from 2009–2013 (Q0–Q17, 30-min cadence), which revealed aperiodic dimming
events of up to ~22% flux drop — far deeper than a transiting planet would
produce.

Kepler-era dip morphology (from Boyajian et al. 2016, MNRAS 457, 3988):
- **D800**: ~5 day asymmetric dip (day ~800), max depth ~15%
- **D1519**: ~4.5 day dip (day ~1519), max depth ~22%
- **D1568**: ~3 day dip (day ~1568), max depth ~8%
- Many smaller dips scattered throughout the light curve

**TESS** observed the star:
- **Sector 14** (2019-07-18 to 2019-08-15, 30-min cadence)
- **Sector 41** (2021-07-23 to 2021-08-19, 10-min cadence)
- **Sector 68** (2023-07-01 to 2023-07-29, 200-s cadence)
- **Sector 95** (2025-06-15 to 2025-07-13, 200-s cadence)

Additional TESS observations in Sectors 14, 41, 68, 95.

## Synthetic data methodology

Generated by `tools/scripts/boyajian_tess_probe.py` with the `--generate-data` flag.

Each synthetic light curve:
- ~28-day duration (one TESS sector)
- 200-second cadence (matching TESS Sector 68+)
- Gaussian white noise (500 ppm — typical TESS single-point precision)
- Optional injected dips with Kepler-morphology (asymmetric shape, 1–5% depth)
- Dip epochs spaced in-block so the period search has a chance to detect them
