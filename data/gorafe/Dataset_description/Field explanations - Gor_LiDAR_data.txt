FIELD			EXPLANATION											CONTENT							

Id			Identification number of the anomaly/possible megalithic structure								

Coor_X			Coordinate X						

Coor_Y			Coordinate Y					

Class			Visual classification of the anomaly, in comparison to the actually preserved dolmens		1 = clear	2 = probable	3 = possible			

Viewshed_M		Viewshed for a radius of 3km around the dolmen, in meters								

Viewshed_Km		Viewshed for a radius of 3km around the dolmen, in kilometers								

Slope			Slope of the pixel in which the anomaly is located, in degrees								

Slope_Av30		Average slope of the terrain around the anomaly with a radius of 30m, in degrees								

Slope_Min30		Minimum slope of the terrain around the anomaly with a radius of 30m, in degrees								

Slope_Max30		Maximum slope of the terrain around the anomaly with a radius of 30m, in degrees								

Slope_Av50		Average slope of the terrain around the anomaly with a radius of 50m, in degrees								

Slope_Min50		Minimum slope of the terrain around the anomaly with a radius of 50m, in degrees								

Slope_Max50		Maximum slope of the terrain around the anomaly with a radius of 50m, in degrees								

Height			Height of the pixel in which the anomaly is located (Coor_Z)								

Height_Av30		Average height of the terrain around the anomaly with a radius of 30m								

Height_Min30		Minimum height of the terrain around the anomaly with a radius of 30m								

Height_Max30		Maximum height of the terrain around the anomaly with a radius of 30m								

Height_Av50		Average height of the terrain around the anomaly with a radius of 50m								

Height_Min50		Minimum height of the terrain around the anomaly with a radius of 50m								

Height_Max50		Maximum height of the terrain around the anomaly with a radius of 50m								

Ori_Terr		Orientation of the terrain (considering the pixel in which the anomaly is located)		1 = N	2 = NE	3 = E	4 = SE	5 = S 	6 = SW	7 = w	8 = NW

Ori_Terr2		Orientation of the terrain (considering the pixel in which the anomaly is located), in degrees								

Dis_Water		Distance to the closer body of water, in meters								

Dis_Gor			Minimum distance to the Gor River, in meters
