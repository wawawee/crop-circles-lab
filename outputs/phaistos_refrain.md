## Honest caveat (unchanged discipline)
- This rests on **2 gaps** for the refrain and N=31 groups on side A. It is a **strong
  hint of metre, not proof** — a short repeated template could also do this.
- It still says **nothing about meaning.** "Hail the king", a hymn, or "2 litres of
  barley, 1 goat" all remain possible. Metre narrows the *genre* (formulaic/poetic over
  ledger-like), not the semantics. No Rosetta → no reading.

## Where this leaves Phaistos
Two independent structural facts now stand: (1) conditional-entropy **z = −14** vs
shuffled (decisively non-random), and (2) a **period-3 refrain** (metrical layout).
Both point to *deliberate, formulaic structure*. Neither crosses into decipherment.
Someone composed something 3,700 years ago with a refrain on a fixed beat — we can
prove the beat; we cannot yet read the words.
