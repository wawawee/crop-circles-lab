Question: how does the diatonic hit rate degrade as the cents bar tightens?

selection bias. If real hit rate *outlives* both nulls across the sweep, 
specific pair-cells are doing work -- not just magnitude structure.

## Survival across tol -- 5-case verdict (strict >)

Real_filt **strict >** Null A p95 in **0/7** tol points  
Real_filt **strict >** Null B p95 in **0/7** tol points  
Real_filt has p(B) < 0.05 in **0/7** tol points  
Null B degenerate (gap_z < threshold OR real_filt ties p95) at **7/7** tol points (per-tol thresholds via --calibrate)

INTERPRETATION (necessary, not sufficient):
  * STRICT > is the right bar: ties with perm_max mean the null has
    saturated, not that real is special. >= over-counts luck.
  * A non-degenerate null with multiple STRICT > + low p(B) is the
    only configuration that earns a quiet lab-internal follow-up.
  -> No STRICT beat AND no sub-0.05 p-value, AND null distribution
     degenerates across most tol points. The real_filt rate sits
     INSIDE the permutation null at every tol -- magnitude
     clustering (close-magnitude constants producing similar
     diatonic densities under any pairing) dominates any apparent
     signal. NOT a defensible 'pair-cells matter' finding.
