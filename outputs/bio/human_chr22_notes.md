## Interpretation

* `Δ_window_mean_H` is the headline test statistic of this probe:
  (mean window entropy of real) minus (mean window entropy of composition-preserving shuffle).
* A 4-bit window entropy close to Shannon's maximum (≈2.0 for uniform bits) means
  the genome is locally random-ish in that window; lower values mean local repetition.
* A NEGATIVE `Δ_window_mean_H` (real < shuffled) is the expected biological signature
  of real genomes: coding regions + repeat families compress regularities
  that a shuffle has to invent by construction. This is biology, not a cipher.
* A POSITIVE `Δ_window_mean_H` would be the 'surprise' signal worth follow-up.
* This probe is intentionally weak on SNPs/intergenic annotation: NC_045512.2 is
  ~99% coding and the chr22 slice is mostly intronic/intergenic so we don't claim
  a coding-vs-intergenic comparison here -- that needs annotation (BED/GFF) and stays
  outside the scope of N1 deliverables.
